Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

OBL Hockey ("we", "us", "our") operates the OBL Hockey web application and mobile app (the "App"), used to manage Ottawa Beer League hockey teams, schedules, stats, and communications. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the App, we collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Birthdate and gender (optional)
  • Player position, skill level/rank, and league selections
  • Availability, vacation periods, and injury status
  • Preferred arenas
  • Game stats you or your commissioner enter (goals, assists, games played)
  • Ratings and written reviews of leagues and players
  • Messages sent through the App's announcement and direct-message features
  • Marketplace/merch listings you create, including photos you upload

We also automatically collect limited technical information, such as push-notification tokens (if you enable notifications), to operate core features of the App.

How We Use Information

  • To operate league scheduling, rosters, stats tracking, and referee/goalie assignment
  • To send you announcements, game reminders, and push notifications you've opted into
  • To display reviews and ratings to other players and commissioners as described in the App
  • To let commissioners manage their league's roster, games, and communications
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the App

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is visible to other users only as intended by the App's features (for example, teammates can see your name and stats; commissioners of your league can see your profile and availability). We use third-party infrastructure providers (such as Supabase for database/authentication hosting and Google for push notifications) solely to operate the App — these providers do not use your data for their own purposes.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. You can request correction or deletion of your data, or full account deletion, at any time by emailing support@oblhockey.com.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult recreational hockey league members and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can review and update most of your profile information at any time from within the App. You can disable push notifications from your device settings or in-app profile settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@oblhockey.com.
